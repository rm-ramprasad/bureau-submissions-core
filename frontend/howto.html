How to submit to the credit bureaus

  1. 1Create Pick the as-of date and the type.
    More detail
    • Only reporting dates: the 9th, 16th, 23rd and month end.
    • Choose DL, DC, AH or RR only for a special file.
  2. 2Upload Upload the workbook. Fix anything marked Blocks.
    More detail
    • Blocks: fix the workbook and upload it again.
    • Note: worth a look; it doesn't stop you.
  3. 3Exceptions Optional: exclude or override. Then submit.
    More detail
    • Give each exception a reason.
    • Submitting locks the workbook and the exceptions.
  4. 4Validate Every record is checked. Review the results.
    More detail
    • Open a category, then View, to see the loans.
    • Download CSV for the operations team.
  5. 5Generate Pick the bureaus. Once per validation.
    More detail
    • Choose every bureau you need now.
    • To make the files again, create a new request.
  6. 6Files Download and upload each to its bureau.
    More detail
    • Bureau file, bureau Excel (consumer), review Excel.
    • Also under Submitted files.

↺ Need to fix something or make the files again? Correct the data, then create a new request.

Reading the results

Not reportedBreaks a bureau rule; left out of the file.
Reported with gapsReported, but a value or section is left out.
Warning onlyReported as it is. Worth a look.

Words used in the app

As-of date
The date the reported data refers to.
Reporting cycle
W1 / W2 / W3 / ME follow the date (9th, 16th, 23rd, month end); DL, DC, AH, RR are chosen for special files.
Finding
One issue in one record, with its effect on the file.
Bureau file
The file for one bureau (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark).
Consumer / Commercial
Loans to individuals / to businesses: separate files and rules.

For administrators
  • Rules & policies is read-only for users; administrators (the Bureau Submission – Admins group) change rules and parameters.
  • Every change is recorded in Audit.
